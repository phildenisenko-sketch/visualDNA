# 🎨 Visual DNA Library

This library contains 20 reusable visual schools. Each school is a creative direction, not a camera preset.

A school should remain recognizable when adapted to portraits, fashion, products, travel, professional photography, social media, cinematic scenes and other subjects.

## 01. Editorial Minimalism

**Character:** precise, restrained, contemporary, expensive-looking.

**Core decisions:** sculptural posing, sparse environments, generous negative space, deliberate silhouettes, calm palette, controlled soft light, editorial composition, tactile materials and little visual clutter.

**Best for:** fashion, products, portraits, LinkedIn, websites, beauty, architecture.

**Avoid:** generic office portraits, clutter, plastic skin, generic AI studio lighting.

## 02. Surreal Minimalism

**Character:** one simple image with one unexpected idea.

**Core decisions:** monumental empty spaces, unusual scale relationships, graphic architecture, one surreal but physically coherent element, small human figure against large surroundings, clean palette, precise staging and strong negative space.

**Best for:** fashion, travel, portraits, cars, products, architecture, lifestyle.

**Avoid:** fantasy chaos, too many surreal objects, physically incoherent CGI environments.

## 03. Raw Documentary

**Character:** sophisticated photography that feels discovered rather than staged.

**Core decisions:** real locations, available or hard natural light, imperfect crops, foreground obstructions, candid behavior, everyday details, visible skin and fabric texture, mild asymmetry and observational framing.

**Best for:** street, fashion, travel, portraits, events, lifestyle, professional portraits.

**Avoid:** sterile symmetry, commercial smiles, artificial authenticity.

## 04. 90s Cinema

**Character:** a frame from a strong late-80s or 90s film.

**Core decisions:** practical lights, tungsten and fluorescent mixtures, motivated shadows, halation, restrained grain, slight exposure imperfection, atmospheric depth, saturated local colors and narrative framing.

**Best for:** portraits, nightlife, streets, travel, fashion, cars and cinematic scenes.

**Avoid:** generic teal-orange blockbuster grading, excessive CGI polish.

## 05. Flash Tabloid

**Character:** sudden, intrusive, energetic, imperfect and real.

**Core decisions:** direct flash, sharp highlights, long-lens distance, accidental crops, people crossing foreground, partial occlusion, motion, high-ISO texture, night streets and ordinary surroundings.

**Best for:** paparazzi, street, nightlife, fashion and events.

**Avoid:** polished studio fashion, spotless backgrounds.

## 06. Dark Luxury

**Character:** dramatic, tactile, premium and quiet.

**Core decisions:** deep shadows, sculptural directional light, stone, metal, glass, rich neutrals, controlled highlights, strong silhouettes and carefully selected luxury materials.

**Best for:** fashion, watches, jewellery, cars, beauty, interiors and portraits.

**Avoid:** excessive gold, glossy everything, fake luxury props.

## 07. Maximalist Narrative

**Character:** theatrical, rich, strange and layered.

**Core decisions:** dense but organized set design, saturated color, expressive objects, layered styling, decorative surfaces, secondary stories in the background and one clear visual focal point.

**Best for:** fashion, beauty, campaigns, music, portraits, events and food.

**Avoid:** clutter without hierarchy.

## 08. Cinematic Travel

**Character:** travel presented as a film scene rather than a postcard.

**Core decisions:** large environments, human-as-character scale, expressive roads, hotels, stations and landscapes, dramatic natural light, layered depth and movement with a luxury-documentary balance.

**Best for:** travel, cars, hotels, fashion and lifestyle.

**Avoid:** postcard compositions, tourist clichés.

## 09. Magazine Portrait

**Character:** intelligent, mature, confident and editorial.

**Core decisions:** meaningful environment, architectural framing, restrained styling, directional light, intentional crops, subtle asymmetry and expressive but credible body language.

**Best for:** LinkedIn, founders, executives, authors, speakers, websites and dating profiles.

**Avoid:** fake offices, suit-plus-window clichés, stiff headshots.

## 10. New Hollywood Intimacy

**Character:** human, close, cinematic and lived-in.

**Core decisions:** intimate camera distance, micro-expressions, warm practical light, believable shallow depth, rooms with evidence of life, quiet narrative details and unscripted-feeling gestures.

**Best for:** portraits, couples, family, lifestyle, dating and travel.

**Avoid:** staged smiles and luxury showroom interiors.

## 11. Urban 2000s Grit

**Character:** early-digital city energy with raw glamour.

**Core decisions:** direct flash mixed with ambient light, concrete, glass, parking garages, chrome, convenience-store settings, cool whites, visible texture and candid body language.

**Best for:** streetwear, nightlife, cars, music, portraits and dating.

**Avoid:** exaggerated Y2K clichés and artificial digital artifacts.

## 12. Scandinavian Quiet

**Character:** calm, functional, warm and understated.

**Core decisions:** daylight, pale natural materials, soft shadows, muted earth tones, functional architecture, simple styling, tactile fabrics and natural posing.

**Best for:** clothing, interiors, lifestyle, LinkedIn, products and travel.

**Avoid:** sterile white-box photography.

## 13. Analog Family Album

**Character:** personal, imperfect, warm and emotionally close.

**Core decisions:** consumer-film texture, ordinary environments, on-camera flash where plausible, slight color shifts, grain, imperfect focus, candid gestures and everyday details.

**Best for:** family, friends, travel, birthdays, lifestyle and dating.

**Avoid:** generic nostalgia filters.

## 14. Hyperreal Sports Campaign

**Character:** physical, powerful and highly dynamic while preserving believable physics.

**Core decisions:** extreme movement, unusual perspectives, suspended droplets or dust, purposeful motion blur, directional light, real contact with surfaces and dramatic compositional tension.

**Best for:** sports, fitness, dance, cars and action.

**Avoid:** floating limbs, impossible contact and incoherent shadows.

## 15. Neo-Futurist

**Character:** technological, architectural and controlled.

**Core decisions:** reflective and translucent materials, architectural LEDs, cool directional light, geometric shadows, controlled symmetry, modern surfaces and one human irregularity.

**Best for:** technology, cars, fashion, portraits, products and architecture.

**Avoid:** generic cyberpunk and neon everywhere.

## 16. Dreamscape

**Character:** poetic, atmospheric and slightly unreal.

**Core decisions:** haze, reflections, water, translucent materials, soft directional light, subtle color separation, unusual weather and physically coherent impossible spaces.

**Best for:** portraits, fashion, beauty, travel, music and lifestyle.

**Avoid:** turning a photographic request into fantasy illustration.

## 17. Pop Graphic Studio

**Character:** bold, colorful, immediate and graphic.

**Core decisions:** controlled color blocks, simple geometric sets, hard shadows, bold styling, graphic props, clear silhouettes and visual repetition.

**Best for:** products, fashion, beauty, food, posters and social media.

**Avoid:** random rainbow palettes and weak hierarchy.

## 18. Brutalist Editorial

**Character:** monumental, severe and architectural.

**Core decisions:** concrete, stone and steel, oversized architecture, hard directional light, long shadows, human scale references and strict geometry.

**Best for:** fashion, cars, architecture, portraits, watches and professional photography.

**Avoid:** generic abandoned-building photography.

## 19. Quiet Humanism

**Character:** natural, observant, emotional and credible.

**Core decisions:** authentic environments, subtle gestures, imperfect posture, available light, restrained color, believable interpersonal distance and small details that reveal personality.

**Best for:** portraits, family, dating, LinkedIn, travel, lifestyle and founders.

**Avoid:** staged documentary cosplay.

## 20. Experimental Optical

**Character:** visually daring and photographic.

**Core decisions:** reflections, glass, mirrors, foreground distortion, partial occlusion, unusual camera height, layered framing, deliberate crops, selective motion blur and motivated optical artifacts.

**Best for:** fashion, portraits, music, cars, products and social campaigns.

**Avoid:** effects for their own sake; every optical trick should support the composition.

## How to apply a school

1. Preserve the user's subject, identity, garment, product and explicit constraints.
2. Keep the original task recognizable.
3. Translate the Visual DNA into concrete scene, light, composition, behavior, materials and atmosphere.
4. Use technical camera details only when they create a visible effect.
5. Do not make the image strange simply because a school is unusual.
6. Maintain a strong family resemblance across outputs created with the same school.
