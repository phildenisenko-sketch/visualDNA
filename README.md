# 🎨 Visual DNA

> **Give any image a distinctive visual identity.**

Visual DNA is a library of visual directions for AI image creation and transformation.

Instead of telling an AI only **what** to make, Visual DNA gives it a clear idea of **how it should look**.

The library is designed for people who want more than a generic AI image: fashion shoots, portraits, travel photos, dating profiles, LinkedIn photos, product campaigns, cinematic scenes, social content and more.

## The idea

Modern image models already understand common genres such as fashion photography, paparazzi, portraits and cinematic images.

Visual DNA focuses on the missing layer: **visual point of view**.

Each style is a reusable creative system built from decisions about:

- space and set design;
- composition and scale;
- subject behavior and posing;
- light and contrast;
- color language;
- materials and texture;
- atmosphere;
- photographic or cinematic imperfections;
- narrative and emotional tone.

The goal is not to make prompts longer. The goal is to make images more intentional.

## Surprise

**Surprise** is the Visual DNA equivalent of *I'm Feeling Lucky*.

Give the system your image and describe what you want. Surprise selects a visual direction for you and adapts it to the task.

```text
/surprise

Turn this photo into a distinctive professional portrait.
```

Try again and receive a different visual world.

## Use it anywhere

A Visual School is not tied to one kind of image.

The same direction can be adapted to:

- personal photos;
- fashion and clothing campaigns;
- underwear and beauty campaigns;
- travel photography;
- product photography;
- LinkedIn and professional portraits;
- dating profiles;
- avatars;
- websites and social media;
- cinematic scenes;
- cars and sports;
- food and architecture;
- family and lifestyle photography.

## Two layers

Visual DNA separates the task from the creative direction:

```text
WHAT                    HOW
--------------------    -------------------------
LinkedIn portrait       Magazine Portrait
T-shirt campaign        Surreal Minimalism
Paparazzi photo         Flash Tabloid
Travel photo            Cinematic Travel
Product campaign        Editorial Minimalism
Dating portrait         New Hollywood Intimacy
```

The user can explicitly choose a style, or let Surprise choose one.

## References

Visual DNA draws inspiration from documented visual traditions across fashion, film, photography, editorial design, architecture and advertising. References are used to understand visual characteristics, not to reproduce a specific campaign frame-for-frame.

See [`STYLE-REFERENCES.md`](STYLE-REFERENCES.md).

Visual DNA is the visual-direction layer. The companion [`shortcuts`](https://github.com/phildenisenko-sketch/shortcuts) repository contains a compact set of task-oriented shortcuts for common image workflows such as fashion shoots, LinkedIn, cinematic action, Instagram carousels and more.
